Indie Game Ideation · Research Brief

12 concepts. 5 picks.
One verdict.

A shortlist of indie game ideas, each stress-tested against three hard filters: a one-verb hook that survives a TikTok thumbnail, a 2-weekend buildable prototype, and structural DNA that already moves units in the Minecraft and Roblox ecosystems. Below: the picks, the bench, and the single call we'd make if you put a gun to our head.

Top 5 picks

#1 · TOP PICK

Fifteen Rounds

Tabletop duel with live ammo. Pull the trigger on instinct.

Buckshot-shape Godot Complexity 2/5 Minecraft DNA
Turn phase Social pressure
Hook
A shotgun, a stranger, fifteen shells you can't see through. Each turn: pull the trigger on them, or on yourself.
Core loop
Inspect live/blank pattern → choose target → resolve damage and item drops → opponent taunts → next round. Sessions are 8–12 minutes; entire game is one screen.
References
Buckshot Roulette (1M copies / 3 wks) Inscryption Reigns
Minecraft / Roblox DNA
Same hidden-info, mod-friendly sandbox a 12-year-old runs on a school tablet. Procedural shell generators + player-made "house rules" mods feed the Roblox UGC appetite without writing a single line of Lua.
2-Weekend Prototype
  1. Weekend 1: 2D table, shotgun sprite, live/blank RNG, 3 items.
  2. Weekend 2: AI opponent that adapts to player bluff frequency, end screen, juice pass.
  3. Stretch: 2-player hot-seat and a workshop of modded shell types.
#2

Web Fishing

A quiet lake where you fish, play guitar, and talk to strangers.

Social sandbox Godot Complexity 2/5 Roblox DNA
Loop Input Soft activity
Hook
An always-online fishing hole where the real loop is talking to whoever's nearby while you wait for a bite.
Core loop
Cast → ambient chat via proximity voice → reel catch → trade at dock → wander to a campfire and strum a guitar with whoever showed up. No fail state, no grind ceiling.
References
Webfishing (solo Godot server) Gartic Phone Animal Crossing
Minecraft / Roblox DNA
This is the Roblox "hang out" archetype in disguise — proximity voice + cosmetic loops + creator-run events. Minecraft players recognise the low-stakes co-op vibe; Roblox players recognise the avatar economy.
2-Weekend Prototype
  1. Weekend 1: lake scene, cast/reel mechanic, 5 fish types, basic netcode.
  2. Weekend 2: proximity voice, guitar with 4 chords, dock trade NPC.
  3. Stretch: cosmetics store + a weekly themed event.
#3

Solitaire City

A roguelike deck. A city on the line. One joker can end you.

Balatro-shape Godot / Unity Complexity 3/5 Minecraft DNA
Hand loop Meta layer Pressure
Hook
Klondike everyone already knows, fused with Balatro's chip-mult math and a meta-city that grows between runs.
Core loop
Deal hand → stack scoring combos → win blinds → spend currency on city districts that buff your next run → survive boss blinds with rule twists (no red, only face cards, double-or-nothing).
References
Balatro (3.5M+, GOTY 2024) Luck be a Landlord Vampire Survivors
Minecraft / Roblox DNA
Same session-bite of Minecraft (one hand during a coffee) with Balatro's "I have to try one more run" addiction. The meta-city is built from real estate players care about — districts unlock cosmetics, leaderboards, and weekly challenges.
2-Weekend Prototype
  1. Weekend 1: Klondike rules, scoring chips, 3 jokers.
  2. Weekend 2: city grid, 4 district types, save/load, share-run code.
  3. Stretch: Steam Workshop for community joker packs.
#4 · TEAM PICK

Kitchen 15

Fifteen-day cooking chaos. Four players. One kitchen. Zero chill.

PlateUp!-shape Unity Complexity 3/5 Roblox DNA
Service loop Pressure Progression
Hook
A roguelike co-op restaurant: each day is one dinner rush, every 15 in-game days is a fresh kitchen with new unlocks.
Core loop
Prep ingredients → cook under fire → plate to ticket → earn tips → spend between days on layout, appliances, and new menu items. Friendly-fire chaos and one shared "fire" gauge force cooperation.
References
PlateUp! (1.5M+ / 1 dev) Overcooked Cooking Simulator
Minecraft / Roblox DNA
PlateUp! proved 1-dev co-op cooking is a real category. Roblox players live for voice-chat chaos with friends; Minecraft players love building the kitchen between runs. Cross-pollenates naturally.
2-Weekend Prototype
  1. Weekend 1: 2-player online, one kitchen, 3 dishes, ticket board.
  2. Weekend 2: day-night cycle, fire gauge, shop, basic progression.
  3. Stretch: 4-player, themed restaurants, Steam Workshop kitchens.
#5

Raid Night

Four heroes. One monster. Friday-night friendships, wrecked.

Asymmetric party Unity + Steam Workshop Complexity 3/5 Roblox DNA
4-party roles Solo antagonist
Hook
Friday-night co-op raids where one friend is the monster. Win or lose, the after-round banter is the content.
Core loop
Pick a loadout → enter a procedurally-stitched dungeon → fight the monster player (or AI if not enough players) → earn loot that mutates your abilities → queue the next raid with the same crew.
References
Lethal Company Among Us Phasmophobia Castle Crashers
Minecraft / Roblox DNA
Steam Workshop modding is the Minecraft slice: player-built dungeons and bosses keep the raid pool fresh for years. Roblox angle: cosmetic loadouts and creator-made monster kits as UGC drops.
2-Weekend Prototype
  1. Weekend 1: 4v1 prototype, one arena, tank/heal/DPS kit, monster AI.
  2. Weekend 2: proc-gen dungeon room, 3 bosses, loot rolls, Steam Workshop loader.
  3. Stretch: ranked mode, replay system, creator storefront.

Verdict

Tasteful. Candid. Opinionated.

If you read nothing else, read this.

Of the twelve concepts, the call comes down to buildability × clarity of hook. Most indie pitches die in the gap between "interesting idea" and "you can describe it in one sentence to a friend on Discord." The five picks above all clear that bar; the bench is solid but each carries one extra risk we wouldn't take on first.

Brick Worlds is the largest opportunity on the board and the worst first project — five-axis complexity, a creator kit to design before any players show up, and a direct collision with UEFN's installed base. Steal a Brainrot is fascinating but it's already won on Roblox; cloning it elsewhere means you're fighting the original's network effect with worse distribution. Honk Protocol has the cleanest comedy hook on the list but no UGC story, which means every minute of player attention has to be earned again each session.

Solitaire City is the smartest single-player bet — Balatro-shaped addiction with a meta-city wrapper. Raid Night is the safest long-tail co-op play, especially once Steam Workshop is live. Web Fishing is the lowest-risk social experiment and the easiest to soft-launch to a Discord. Fifteen Rounds is the purest two-weekend prototype you can ship in this document.

Recommended next step: pick two.

If we had to scope a 2-person team for the next 4–6 weeks, we'd start with Fifteen Rounds in Godot — to validate prototype velocity and a clean, screenshot-able hook. If we had a team willing to commit 4–6 months to a deeper experience with live-ops potential, we'd greenlight Kitchen 15 in Unity — co-op roguelike cooking is a proven category, and one developer already proved it ships with PlateUp!.

Pick #1 · Velocity

Fifteen Rounds · Godot

2-person, 2-weekend prototype. Tests whether the team can ship a tight, screenshot-able loop before committing to anything heavier.

Pick #2 · Depth

Kitchen 15 · Unity

4–6 month commitment. Co-op roguelike cooking, proven category, Room for creator kitchens and live-ops.
