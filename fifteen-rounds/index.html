How it works

  • 3 rounds per match. Each round loads 4 live + 4 blank shells (with a possible ±1 twist).
  • You always go first. On your turn: Shoot the dealer or shoot yourself.
  • A live shell hurts whoever it hits and ends your turn. A blank fired at yourself keeps your turn going.
  • Items: Magnifier (peek), Handcuffs (skip the next opponent turn), Cigarettes (heal 1 HP). Each side draws up to 2 per round.
  • First side to win 2 rounds takes the match.
Single-player. No friends. No mercy.
