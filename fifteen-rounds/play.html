How to play

You're in a 3-round shotgun duel against the dealer. Each round, the gun loads 4 live + 4 blank shells (sometimes 5/3 with a ±1 twist). You always go first in round 1.

  • Live → hit the target, deal 1 HP, and your turn ends.
  • Blank → shoot yourself: you keep the turn (chain blanks for damage).
  • Blank → shoot dealer: their turn, no damage.

Items (up to 2 per side per round, unused items persist):

  • Magnifier — peek the current shell.
  • Handcuffs — skip the opponent's next turn.
  • Cigarettes — heal 1 HP (max 4).

Win a round by dropping the other side to 0 HP before shells run out. If shells run out, the side with more HP wins; a tie is a draw. First to 2 round wins takes the match.
